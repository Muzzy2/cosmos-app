Let it listen

Your music becomes the weather: bass bends gravity, beats ripple the dust, the universe retunes to your song's key and locks to its tempo, and creatures who share its notes are fed.

Songs stay on this device only (nothing is uploaded). Pick several at once for a playlist; the lock screen and headphone buttons control it. Drop files or a folder anywhere.

Pick the tab and tick “Share tab audio”. DRM-protected players may share silence.

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube?

They stream locked (DRM) audio, so no app can hand the sound to COSMOS. Instead: play the music on a speaker, TV, laptop or another phone, then tap “Listen through the microphone” here and put this phone nearby. It hears the room, finds the beat and the key, and plays along. Songs you own as files (mp3, m4a, wav, flac, from the Files app or iCloud Drive) work directly with “Add songs”.

Your sounds

Give the creatures your own voices: pick a few samples from your library (one-shots, bells, plucks, pads). A note name in the file name (“pad C3.wav”) sets its pitch, otherwise C4. They stay on this device and are never published.
